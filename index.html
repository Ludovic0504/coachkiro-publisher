Coach Kiro Publisher

Coach Kiro Publisher is a personal publishing tool that lets a content creator send their own original fitness videos to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The creator selects a video from their computer and writes the caption.
  3. The creator chooses the privacy level and confirms.
  4. The app sends the video to TikTok using the Content Posting API.

Nothing is ever published without the creator's explicit confirmation.
